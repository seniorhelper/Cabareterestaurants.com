Why the north coast is busy

The Dominican Republic welcomed a record 11.19 million visitors in 2024, including 8.54 million by air, according to the Ministry of Tourism figures released by the Presidency. Cabarete sits a short drive from Puerto Plata’s Gregorio Luperón airport.

Many visitors here stay a week or a month for the wind and the waves, so they eat out often and they ask their phones first. A listing that shows your food, hours and location is how you stay on their short list.

Questions owners ask

Is a basic listing really free?

Yes. Restaurants in Cabarete can request an accurate basic listing on Cabarete Restaurants for free: name, area, cuisine, price range, hours and links. We verify you’re the owner or manager before changes go live. Featured and Menu Pro are optional upgrades, and nothing you buy changes whether we keep you listed.

What is the difference between Featured and Menu Pro?

Featured ($29 a month) gives you a top spot on Cabarete Restaurants with a badge, a photo, a pitch and your link. Menu Pro ($49 a month or $490 a year) includes the same Featured placement plus a hosted, Google-readable menu page, a full menu update every quarter and NFC table tags, all done for you.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No, and we won’t pretend it does. We promise the work: a clearly labeled top placement, your photo and link, and with Menu Pro a menu that search engines can read and that we keep current. How many diners choose you still depends on your food, reviews and service.

How fast does it go live?

Usually within 48 hours of getting your photo, pitch and link. Menu Pro menu pages usually go live within 48 hours of receiving your menu. We confirm you run the restaurant first, so a quick reply to our welcome email keeps things moving.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime online and stay live until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Menu Pro yearly is one payment of $490 for twelve months, which is two months free compared with paying monthly.

Can my listing be in Spanish and English?

Yes. Send your pitch in either language, or both, and we’ll match the guide’s bilingual layout. Menu Pro menus can show dish names in Spanish with English descriptions, which helps visitors order and helps search engines understand each dish.
