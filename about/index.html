About Cabarete Restaurants

Who runs this guide, how it is paid for and how we keep it accurate.

We built Cabarete Restaurants because good, honest information about where and what to eat in Cabarete and Sosúa was scattered across forums, old blog posts and listings that never get updated. This site pulls it together, cites where each fact came from and dates it.

The guide is published by Zach Wennstedt through Eye To Ad Media (Denver, Colorado). No restaurant group, resort or tourism company owns or directs it.

A few businesses support the site with paid placements. You will always see the word Sponsored next to them. Paid or not, we never invent ratings, prices, hours or reviews, and we remove places we can no longer confirm.

How to reach us

Spotted a closure, a wrong address or a place we should add? Use the contact page. We read every message and correct mistakes quickly.

Read our editorial policy for the full research method.
