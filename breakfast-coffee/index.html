Breakfast and coffee in Cabarete

The wind usually builds through the day, so Cabarete eats breakfast early. These are the places locals and regulars start with.

Mornings are Cabarete’s quiet hours. The bay is calm, cafés fill with people planning their day, and remote workers open laptops before the beach gets busy. Eating early and light leaves you ready for the water when the wind arrives.

The cafés

  • Cabarete Coffee Company is run as a social business of the Mariposa DR Foundation, which educates and empowers girls in Cabarete. Profits support the foundation, and graduates of its center train in the café. Good coffee, smoothies and sandwiches.
  • Vagamundo Coffee & Waffles does sweet and savory waffles, smoothie bowls and coffee with indoor and outdoor seating. It has moved before, so check the current address.
  • Fresh Fresh is the healthy option for bowls, juices and lighter plates.
  • Mojito Bar serves breakfast on the beach for a sea-view start.

A Dominican breakfast

For something local, order mangú (mashed green plantain) with sautéed onions, and los tres golpes: fried salami, fried cheese and eggs. Fried cheese and salami also turn up beside cornmeal or yuca fritters, as in the photo above. Wash it down with sweet Dominican coffee or a fresh juice such as chinola (passion fruit).

Working remotely

Several cafés are popular with digital nomads. We do not list Wi-Fi speed or power outlets unless a café publishes them, so ask before you settle in, buy something every hour or two, and avoid the lunch rush at small places.

Timing

  • Cafés open early; arrive before 9 a.m. for the calmest tables.
  • By late morning, kiters head for the water and cafés empty out.
  • Dominican plates at comedores are lunch food (noon to 2 p.m.).

Good to know

Is Cabarete Coffee Company a charity?

It is a social business run by the Mariposa DR Foundation, a Cabarete non-profit. Profits support the foundation’s programs for girls.

What is a typical Dominican breakfast?

Mangú with onions and los tres golpes: fried salami, fried cheese and eggs, often with coffee or fresh juice.
