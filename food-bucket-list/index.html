How the planner decides

The planner uses the same data as our restaurant directory. Every place has editor’s tags taken from its sources: the area, the kind of food, the meals a source mentions (for example, a café described as a breakfast spot) and a vibe when a guide or the business describes one, such as "on the water" or "date night". Choosing an area keeps results there and adds the next area over at a lower score.

Scores come only from your choices. A sponsored business is labeled but never ranked higher. If you ask for vegetarian food, places with a sourced plant-based focus rise to the top; if none match, the planner says so and lists dishes that are typically meat-free.

"Make it an evening" picks a drinks stop, dinner and a café or gelato stop in the same area when we have sourced options. Your selections stay in the address bar so you can share them, and your saved list lives only in your browser. Print it or email it to yourself before you head out.

Sample plans

Kiter’s day: Kite Beach, Café & breakfast, Barefoot casual, Breakfast. You get La Mesa Taína on the water plus café picks in town for the morning.

Sunset date: Cabarete town & bay, Seafood, Date night, Dinner. La Casita de Papi leads, with Bliss and Le Bistro for a dressier evening.

Plant-based: Cabarete town & bay, Vegetarian-friendly, Vegetarian. Tuvà and Fresh Fresh rise to the top.

Sosúa night: Sosúa, Seafood, On the water. The Waterfront over Playa Alicia comes first, with Casa Veintiuno for a polished dinner.

Before you go

  • Check opening hours: we never publish hours a business has not published.
  • Carry cash for small places.
  • Use the bill calculator to understand tax and service.
