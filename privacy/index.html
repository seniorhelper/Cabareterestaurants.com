Privacy policy

Short version: we keep as little as possible. Updated October 2026.

Information you give us

Messages sent through our contact form travel through FormSubmit to our inbox, along with the name and email you entered. We use them only to answer you. There are no user accounts on this site.

Information stored on your device

Interactive features such as the meal planner or the saved list keep your selections in your browser's local storage. Nothing from those tools is uploaded. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Ads and cookies

We show ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners show you ads based on visits to this and other sites. To turn off personalized ads, go to Google Ads Settings; to opt out of other vendors' cookies, visit aboutads.info.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized advertising cookies are used.

Third-party services

Pressing "Show map" loads OpenStreetMap tiles and the Leaflet script from cdnjs. Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Each service sees a normal web request from your browser.
