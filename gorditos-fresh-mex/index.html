Gordito’s Fresh Mex, Cabarete: menu and directions

Our featured partner. This page is a paid placement; the menu and prices below come from Gordito’s own menu and may change.

What it is

Fresh Mexican-inspired food made from scratch: tacos, burritos and bowls, with the Mambo fish taco as the signature. Taco Tuesday brings special taco pricing.

Find it

Plaza Ocean Dream, Cabarete. The plaza can be tricky to find on foot, so use a map app. Open the location in Google Maps.

Phone (from the business): 829-844-3434 · gorditosfreshmex.com

Payment: cash only, and taxes are included in menu prices, according to the menu.
