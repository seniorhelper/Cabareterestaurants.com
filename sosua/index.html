Where to eat in Sosúa

A short drive west of Cabarete, Sosúa has its own beaches, a lively center and a handful of restaurants worth the trip.

Sosúa sits between Puerto Plata and Cabarete on the coast road. Its main beach curves around a sheltered bay, and the smaller Playa Alicia is known for sunset views. The town has a long history of settlers from abroad, which shows in its mix of European, Caribbean and international kitchens.

Picks by occasion

  • Sunset dinner: the Waterfront restaurant overlooking Playa Alicia is the place most local guides name first for oceanfront dining and Caribbean seafood.
  • Special occasion: Casa Veintiuno is the usual pick for a polished, European-style dinner.
  • Something different: Mosha’s Reggae Lounge for Jamaican jerk chicken and oxtail.
  • Breakfast and coffee: Dolci & Sapori.
  • Family dinner: NLB Nelson’s Lounge & Bistro near the center has an open-air terrace; Fodor’s includes it in its North Coast picks.
  • Varied menu: Margot Restaurant.

Halfway back toward Cabarete, the Beach Club at Sea Horse Ranch is an upscale oceanfront option inside the resort, with live music nights according to Fodor’s.

Getting there

The drive from Cabarete takes about twenty minutes by taxi. Guaguas (shared minibuses) run along the coast road during the day and cost very little. At night, arrange a taxi back with the restaurant.

Tips

  • Book the sunset tables on weekends.
  • Some Sosúa listings in guides are years old; we include only places that recent sources still describe, and we suggest checking hours.
  • Puerto Plata is further west: see our sister guide.
